# GARUDA
A slowly fast F3F Glider

Lets describe this glider as a VJX.glide on steroids. Why? The current philosophie in F3F competition is to have 2 types of gliders in your lineup. One for very fast conditions (40-45s and below) and one for light to mid conditions. Since the TAIPAN (based on VJX.race and the JX-GS3 airfoils) is my current glider for fast conditions i am in dire need of a modern glider purely designed for light conditions. The concept of JX-GT airfoils (MAMBA GTE, ENKI) is a step in the right direction, but it is not maxed out for international competition tasks. To simply copy a VJX.glide would be boring too and no progress would be made. So what needs to be changed, to get even better in said light conditions? Turns out the factor of aspect ratio seems to massively helps (at least theoretically) in these conditions, so this needs to be tried. I will document this process in this project.

Based on older designs, like the Caracho 3.1 (my first molded glider with 3.1m wingspan) and the Caracho 3300 from the 90s i import this concept into modern times with modern airfoils.

The result should be a glider perfectly suited for slow F3F (hence a slowly fast glider) and perfect for hobby use too.
